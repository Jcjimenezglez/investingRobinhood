## What changed

We are **not** running two books. We split **research** from **execution**.

| Layer | Who | Job |
|-------|-----|-----|
| 1 | Ackman / Pershing Square process | Quality checklist (durable business, not zero overnight) |
| 2 | Kevin Xu filter | Whether to buy, one name, support + catalyst, sell +20–30% |

Pershing Q1 2026 13F (~$13.7B, filed 2026-05-15) is a **hunting ground**, not a shopping list. BN / AMZN / UBER / MSFT / QSR / META / HHH stay as quality examples. They do not get bought because they are on the 13F.

## What we will not do again

- Three names at once
- 3–12 month “fair value” holds
- BUY because Ackman added
- Skip “don’t chase” because the name is “high quality”

MSFT +30% was an **early** quality name with weeks to the Q. AMZN +11% on the same Ackman hold did not hit the swing target. Xu still exits the winner and does not wait a year for the laggard.

## Live book

Agentic remains **cash** (~$117.45) until a name passes **both** a decent quality check **and** Xu timing.
